## Terminology
# Country
The nation by which the coin is manufactured.

# Denomination
The category of monetary value to which the coin belongs.

# Series
The cosmetic subcategory of denomination bearing no variation in monetary value.

# Year
The year presented on the coin that represents the year it was produced.

# Mint
A letter presented on a coin representing the factory it was produced in.

# Composition
The material which, on Earth, composes the most mass within the coin, with its relative percentage to other materials in the coin.

# Weight
The expected weight of the coin on Earth in grams, based on database-referenced information.

# Diameter
The diameter or twice the radius of the coin.

# Thickness
The thickness of the coin from its side profile.

# Condition
An approximate wear rating of the coin.
